Gable Guardians: a playable Solana MMORPG where your character is actually yours

Most "GameFi" pitches start with a token and promise a game later. Gable Guardians does it the other way around: it starts with a game you can log into right now.

Gable Guardians is a 2D pixel-art MMORPG on Solana. You play a gargoyle — not a fixed class, but a build you shape by spending stat points and choosing a weapon. You hunt across eight combat zones and four elemental cities, craft weapons and armor, enchant them with elements, run dungeons, fight other players, join guilds, and trade. The project describes itself as an open alpha (version v0.3.231) that launched on December 12, 2025, developed by @elchuo160 (homepage, documentation).

Who it is for

This is aimed at people who want a game first and a wallet second. There is no download: you play in a browser at play.gableguardians.xyz, or on the Solana Seeker and PlaySolana PSG1 devices. You can sign in with an external Solana wallet or with just an email through Privy, which creates an embedded wallet. The ownership model is the point: your gargoyle, weapons, dragon armor and skills are minted as compressed NFTs (cNFTs) via Metaplex, and gargoyles awakened at The Altar become unique 1:1 Metaplex Core assets. They live in your wallet and can be sold on Tensor or through the in-game Zyla marketplace — not held by the studio.

What exists today vs. what is planned

Available now: leveling to a current cap of 100, forging and upgrading weapons, three jewelry quest lines, a ten-element enchanting system with status effects, two group dungeons (Dragon and Kongor Sanctuary), PvP arenas with betting, guilds and guild wars, daily missions, and a live seasonal event — the Halloween 2026 "Morwen" event running October 1–31, 2026 with a 20,000 SKR + 200,000 PLAY reward pool. Planned: a maximum level of 1,000, growing at roughly 100 levels per year. In other words, the foundation is shipped; the long tail is a roadmap.

The token side

Gable Guardians runs a multi-token economy: PLAY, SKR, GABLE, SOL, and a gold-backed GOLD. Most moment-to-moment rewards flow through PLAY/SKR, while $GABLE is the community and premium currency — quoted for Halloween pacts, NFT mints, weapon and jewelry upgrades, and dungeon entries (mint fees are shown as SOL/GABLE equivalents). One promising aspect

The ownership loop is genuine and already shipping. Progress you earn is represented by transferable on-chain assets, a marketplace is wired to a real external venue (Tensor), and the game runs on mainstream Solana hardware — not a closed demo. For a first-time visitor, that combination — a working game, real assets, multiple entry platforms — is a meaningful step up from a token with a whitepaper.

One meaningful risk / unanswered question

The numbers are tiny, and that is the honest caveat. As of 18 October 2026, the most liquid GABLE pool (Meteora GABLE/SOL) held roughly $11,980 of liquidity with about $506 in 24-hour volume; the Raydium GABLE/ESF pool added ~$3,523 liquidity and ~$306 volume, for a fully diluted valuation near $23,000. The price was down ~6.5% in 24 hours on the main pair (DexScreener). Because most in-game rewards are denominated in PLAY/SKR/SOL and GABLE sits on the premium edges, the open question is how much durable demand and how many sinks GABLE actually captures — and whether such a thin float and liquidity can support the premium purchases the design relies on. As an alpha, balance and progression are also subject to change.
