KNOTS on STONK: the Solana token that pays you in its own mirror image

Most tokens need a whitepaper. KNOTS needs one sentence: "$STONK spelled backward is $KNOTS." The project — KNOTS ON STONK LLC, registered in Wyoming — launched on the StonkFun Launchpad on Solana, and its documented utility is that joke turned into a mechanism.

A 3% transfer tax on every $KNOTS transfer, on any venue, is collected and paid to holders in $STONK, pro-rata, with roughly 2.5% deducted to cover the network costs of distributing and supporting operations. Rewards accumulate until they are worth distributing, then go to wallets holding at least $20 of $KNOTS at that moment. That is the entire machine, and a first-time visitor understands it in seconds — which is the point.

One promising aspect

The promising aspect is the reward engine's track record. It has paid continuously and verifiably on-chain for a month — more than 1.5 million payouts — and the trading cup turns pure holding into a participation loop. That is more than most meme tokens manage.

One meaningful risk

The meaningful risk is structural, not cosmetic. A 3% tax plus roughly 2.5% in distribution overhead means a round trip costs about 5.5–6%, and the payoff is denominated in $STONK — a second speculative asset whose value floats independently. "Holders earn $STONK" only creates durable value if $STONK itself holds value; otherwise the reward is churn.

The open question: with 24h volume near $71K across pools, what keeps more than $1.6M in past payouts from becoming steady sell pressure on a thin book? The number worth watching is the ratio of rewards paid to fresh volume — not the headline payout total.
